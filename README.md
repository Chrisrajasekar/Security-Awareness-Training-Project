## Executive Summary
This repository contains an end-to-end framework for an enterprise-grade **Security Awareness & Behavioral Risk Management Program** engineered for regulated financial institutions and multi-segment enterprises. 

Designed to shift corporate security culture from compliance-focused checkmarks to measurable risk reduction, this program bridges technical threat detection (SOC telemetry/Incident Response) with targeted, role-based behavioral interventions. The controls and metrics within this repository explicitly support compliance with **OSFI Guideline B-13 (Technology and Cyber Risk Management)**, **ISO/IEC 27001 (Control 6.3)**, **NIST CSF 2.0 (PR.AT)**, and **PCI DSS (Requirement 12.6)**.

## 📐 Architecture & Feedback Loop

The core methodology relies on an **Incident-to-Training Feedback Loop**, converting real-time SOC telemetry into rapid targeted micro-learning interventions within 48 hours of detection.

### Emerging Threat & AI Defense Playbooks

#### Scenario A: AI Voice Cloning & Deepfake Vishing
* **Threat Profile:** Threat actors cloning executive voices to authorize urgent wire transfers or credential resets.
* **Control Mechanism:** Implementation of mandatory **Out-of-Band (OOB) Authentication Protocols** via secondary trusted channels.
* **Training Deliverable:** Interactive webinar demonstrating real-time voice synthesis tools, followed by controlled vishing drills for finance personnel.

#### Scenario B: Quishing (QR Code Phishing)
* **Threat Profile:** QR codes embedded in spoofed emails bypassing Secure Email Gateways (SEGs).
* **Control Mechanism:** Mobile Device Management (MDM) integration paired with "Scan Verification" guidelines.
* **Training Deliverable:** Byte-sized infographic campaign on recognizing suspicious QR codes on corporate vs. personal devices.

### Cybersecurity Awareness Month Campaign Plan
An annual enterprise engagement framework designed to elevate security culture across global business segments:

Week 1: The Human Firewall – Interactive kick-off featuring executive keynotes and Phish-Reporting Leaderboards.

Week 2: Defending Against AI Threats – Live ethical hacking demonstration showcasing deepfakes, voice cloning, and AI phishing.

Week 3: Cyber Security at Home & On the Go – Best practices for hybrid/remote workers, personal device security, and family cybersecurity.

Week 4: Gamified Threat Hunt – Enterprise-wide "Spot the Phish" interactive challenge with recognition awards for top security champions.
